Modular Playpen Setup

Building a custom modular bunny playpen using durable cardboard panels or wire grids is an excellent, cost-effective way to give your indoor rabbits room to thrive

Pest Protection:Ensure your structure has solid borders and sits in an area free from drafts. Keep food elevated and floors swept clear of stray hay scraps to protect against unwanted pests like rats.
