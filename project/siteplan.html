Website Plan: The Rabbit Burrow

Site Name

Name: The Rabbit Burrow

Why I chose it: I chose this name because a burrow is a rabbit's natural home. It fits perfectly since my website is all about keeping pet rabbits safe and healthy indoors.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to help rabbit owners take better care of their pets. It will give clear info on what foods are safe to feed them and how to build a safe indoor playpen that keeps out pests like rats.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • Can I safely feed my rabbit fresh mango leaves or other greens from the yard?
  • How can I build a cardboard playpen that actually keeps my rabbit safe from pests?

Color Schema

I chose natural, earthy colors that match a pet care theme:

  • Primary Color: #2c5e3b (Forest Green) - Used for all headers.
  • Background Color: #f9f9f9 (Off-White) - Used for the page background.
  • Accent Color: #8ebd9e (Sage Green) - Used for borders and accent lines.

Typography

Heading Font: Arial, sans-serif (Clean and easy to read for titles)

Body Text Font: Georgia, serif (A nice, simple font for reading paragraphs)

Site Structure & Wireframes

The site will have 3 pages total:

  • Home Page (index.html): Introduces indoor rabbit husbandry and housing using a full-width visual hero layout.
  • Diet Guide (diet.html): The page with the JS array to filter safe/unsafe foods.
  • Contact Form (setup.html): A form where users can ask questions or share their setup.
